# Stakeholder Brief — Interest Rates and U.S. Stock Market Risk

**Audience:** Long-term individual investor  
**Decision Supported:** Whether to review personal risk tolerance and portfolio allocation

## Context

Interest-rate changes, rising market volatility, and stock-market movements can be difficult for individual investors to interpret together. This project provides historical context rather than trading recommendations.

## What You Will Receive

- A monthly summary of the effective federal funds rate, VIX, and S&P 500 trends
- Charts showing historical relationships among the indicators
- Plain-language notes about market-risk conditions
- A clear statement of assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations

## Assumptions & Constraints

- Historical patterns do not guarantee future results.
- The analysis uses public data and may not capture all drivers of market behavior.
- This work is educational and is not personalized investment advice.

## Key Risks

- Correlation does not prove causation.
- Volatility can change quickly due to events not included in the analysis.
- VIX does not predict whether stocks will go up or down.
